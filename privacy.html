Privacy policy

Moodboard · last updated 24 October 2026

The short version

Moodboard keeps your boards on your own device. If you turn on Google Drive sync, it also keeps a copy in your own Google Drive. Nobody else, including whoever runs this site, can see your boards.

What the app stores and where

Everything you add (boards, notes, images, links, files, voice notes and settings) is stored in your browser's storage on your device. It isn't sent to any server run by this app. This website only delivers the app's code.

Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose Connect Google Drive, you sign in with your Google account and the app asks for the drive.file permission. This lets the app create a "Moodboard" folder in your Drive and read and change only the files it creates there. It can't see any other files in your Drive.

The app uses this access only to sync your boards between your devices, save weekly backups, and create board copies when you choose Share a copy. Your data goes directly between your device and Google; it doesn't pass through any other server. The app also reads your Google account's email address to show which account is connected.

The sign-in token is kept on your device for about an hour and is never shared. You can disconnect at any time in Settings, and remove the app's access completely at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in your Drive stay there until you delete them.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Board copies made with Share a copy are viewable by anyone with the link. Delete the file from the Moodboard › shared folder in Drive to stop sharing it.

Analytics and advertising

None. The app has no tracking, analytics, cookies for tracking, or adverts, and your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Delete everything to remove all data from a device, or clear the site's data in your browser. Delete the Moodboard folder in Google Drive to remove the synced copy.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the person who shared this app with you, using the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen.
